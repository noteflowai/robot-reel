01 / A controlled release

Only bending changes.

Each 0.96 × 0.64 m sheet has 117 vertices and 192 triangles. Two columns are fixed. Every free vertex has mass 0.01 kg; gravity is 9.81 m/s². All three start at rest.

No collisions, self-contact or learned policy. The dashed outline shows the initial sheet.

03 / From solver to scene

Edit it in Blender.

The USD stores original mesh points and velocities at every sample. Set Blender to 30 fps, then import. Frame 1 is the untouched initial state. Native readback checks the deformation.
