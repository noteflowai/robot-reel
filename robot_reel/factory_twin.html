Factory Twin Lab / campus × line × energy

Same shift. Same failure.
Close the loop.

A simulated plant streams noisy, lossy telemetry to its digital twin. The twin mirrors the line, estimates hidden spindle wear, forecasts the billed power demand, tests what-if plans on its own model and sends commands back. Replay the shift with the loop closed, or with the same twin in shadow mode, where it only advises.

  1. PlantSix stations, 5 buffers, 3 AMRs, hall climate, PV, EV chargers. 1 s steps.
  2. TelemetryPLC, CNC, energy, hall and AMR packets every 5 s. 2–3% lost; one sample of latency.
  3. SyncThe twin mirrors buffers, states and AMR poses from the packets it actually received.
  4. EstimateExtended Kalman filter on vibration → CNC 2 wear and wear rate.
  5. PredictEvery 5 min: 14 what-if runs of its own line model over 90 min. 15-min demand forecast every minute.
  6. Decide & actService, feed derate, setpoint and EV caps. Applied after 5 s — or only logged.
