Robot Reel GitHub showcase

The cover, animated montage and scene thumbnails are derived from Robot Reel's
published recordings. No new policy behavior or physics is generated.
The panels show independent runs; each panel holds its last sample after its
own recording ends. This is a preview, not a synchronized experiment or a
complete evidence bundle. Source frame mappings and SHA-256 hashes are in
manifest.json. Open each demo for its complete recording and verification.

The VLA imagery depicts LIBERO/robosuite assets. Preserve the full upstream
attribution and applicable terms in ../vla/NOTICE.txt (also available in the
repository at licenses/VLA-MEDIA-NOTICE.txt). Robot Reel's Apache-2.0 code
license does not relicense those assets.

The director scene and Newton geometry are authored procedurally in Robot Reel.
The Microduck image linked separately from the README retains its upstream
noncommercial/share-alike terms and its own media notice; it is not in this montage.

social.png (the repository's social preview / Open Graph image) is derived solely
from ../chaos/poster.png, the Butterfly Lab poster: twelve recorded Newton worlds,
measured poses, depth representing time. No other recording or asset is used and
no new physics is generated. Rebuild it with scripts/build_social_preview.py.
